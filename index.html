How it works

Your webcam tracks the angle of your head in real time. A deliberate nod scrolls — anything else is ignored.

1

One click. Available for Chrome, Edge, Brave, and Atlas.

2

3

Lift your chin to advance, drop to go back, stick out your tongue to like the reel. Adjust sensitivity with the slider until it feels right.

Inside the controls

The overlay panel gives you full control. Here's what each piece does.

Pitch bar

Shows the angle of your head in real time. A scroll fires the moment the green dot crosses one of the threshold marks — up to go forward, down to go back.

Sensitivity slider

Higher means a smaller nod triggers a scroll; lower needs a more deliberate one. Default 5 works for most people. The threshold marks move in real time.

Enable / Disable Tracking

Required on first use to request camera permission. Click Disable to instantly cut the camera feed — there's no background recording.

Minimize ( − ) / Expand ( + )

Click the − to collapse the panel down to just its title bar while tracking keeps running. Click the + to bring the full panel back. Great when you want the overlay out of the way without losing hands-free scrolling.

Drag to move

Grab the "NOD SCROLL" header to drag the overlay anywhere on the page. Helpful if it ever sits on top of a video control you want to click.

Free trial counter

Shows how many free scrolls remain today, counted across every supported site combined. Resets at midnight. One $0.99 unlock removes the daily limit forever, on up to 3 devices.

Heads up

After dragging or clicking inside the Nod Scroll panel, click once on the reel video before nodding. Some sites (YouTube Shorts in particular) only respond to scrolls when the page itself has focus — not when the overlay does.

Privacy

Your camera feed never leaves your device.

All face tracking happens locally in your browser using Google's MediaPipe model. We do not record, store, transmit, or have access to your camera feed in any way. No analytics, no telemetry, no servers involved.

The only data persisted is your unlock code and free-trial counter, stored locally in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to us or any third party.
