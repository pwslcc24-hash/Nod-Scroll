Privacy

Your camera feed never leaves your device.

All face tracking happens locally in your browser using Google's MediaPipe model. We do not record, store, transmit, or have access to your camera feed in any way. No analytics, no telemetry, no servers involved.

The only data persisted is your unlock code and free-trial counter, stored locally in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to us or any third party.

No. Face tracking runs entirely in your browser using on-device machine learning. The video feed never leaves your computer.

Does it work on phones?

Not yet — mobile browsers don't support extensions in a way that lets us run there. We're looking at iOS Safari and a possible standalone app.
