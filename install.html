How to install

  1. Unzip the file you just downloaded. You'll get a folder called extension.
  2. Open your browser and go to chrome://extensions (in the URL bar).
  3. Toggle Developer mode on (top-right corner of the page).
  4. Click Load unpacked (top-left button).
  5. Pick the extension folder you unzipped.
  6. Visit any reel page — Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, or Instagram Reels — and look for the Nod Scroll panel in the bottom-right.
  7. Click Enable Tracking and grant camera permission. Nod up to scroll, down to go back.
Privacy: your webcam feed never leaves your device. All face tracking runs locally in your browser. No uploads, no telemetry, no servers.
